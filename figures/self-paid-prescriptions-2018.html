Entirely self-paid prescriptions cost Americans $21.5 billion in 2018

Fills where insurance contributed nothing — no Medicare, Medicaid, private, VA, TRICARE or other third-party dollar. They are 24% of all prescription fills and carry 45% of every out-of-pocket dollar Americans spend on drugs. But the fills and the dollars sit in opposite places.
