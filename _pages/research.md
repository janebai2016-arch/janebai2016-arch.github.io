## Prescription Drugs

Prescriptions where insurance pays nothing — no Medicare, Medicaid, private, VA or other third-party dollar. Roughly a quarter to a third of all fills, and about half of everything Americans pay out of pocket for drugs.

- [Entirely self-paid prescriptions, 2023](/figures/self-paid-prescriptions-2023.html) — $24.0 billion; 31% of fills, 50% of out-of-pocket spending
- [Entirely self-paid prescriptions, 2018](/figures/self-paid-prescriptions-2018.html) — $21.5 billion; 24% of fills, 45% of out-of-pocket spending
