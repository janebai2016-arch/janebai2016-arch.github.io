Jane Bai is a Ph.D. student at the RAND School of Public Policy and an
assistant policy researcher at RAND. Her research interests include health
care delivery, global health, and prescription drug pricing, in both the
United States and China.

Prior to joining RAND, she worked at the Harvard T. H. Chan School of Public
Health as a visiting scientist and at the China Medical Board. She has
experience in policy intervention design, implementation, and evaluation. She
also worked on new drug development, access, and pricing in the private
sector.

She has an M.P.P. from the Kennedy School of Government at Harvard University
and a B.A. in economics from Stanford University.
